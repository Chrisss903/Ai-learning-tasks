1. PARTIES

This Service Agreement is entered into between AlphaTech Solutions
and BetaWorks Pvt. Ltd.

2. EFFECTIVE DATE

The agreement will remain valid for a period of three years
from the effective date.

maintenance, and technical support services to BetaWorks Pvt. Ltd.

30 days written notice to the other party.

technical, and financial information shared during the
term of the agreement.

7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

Any software specifically developed for BetaWorks Pvt. Ltd.
under this agreement will belong to BetaWorks Pvt. Ltd.
after all applicable payments have been completed.

8. GOVERNING LAW

This agreement shall be governed by the applicable laws
of India.

9. GENERAL TERMS

Any changes to this agreement must be made through a
written amendment approved by both parties.
