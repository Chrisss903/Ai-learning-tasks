PLAYER CODE OF CONDUCT
For RAG Training Purposes Only

Document ID: COC-2026

1. APPLICATION

This Code of Conduct applies to every registered player, coach,
and support staff member of a Premier Cricket League franchise.

2. LEVEL 1 OFFENCES

A Level 1 offence is minor dissent, excessive appealing, or
using an audible obscenity. The sanction is a warning or a fine
of up to 25 percent of the Match Fee, plus 1 demerit point.

3. LEVEL 2 OFFENCES

A Level 2 offence is showing serious dissent at an umpire's
decision, deliberate physical contact between players, or
throwing the ball at a player. The sanction is a fine of 50 to
100 percent of the Match Fee, plus 2 demerit points.

4. LEVEL 3 OFFENCES

A Level 3 offence is intimidating an umpire or threatening
language towards a player or official. The sanction is a
suspension of one match, plus 4 demerit points.

5. LEVEL 4 OFFENCES

A Level 4 offence is any act of violence, use of a racial or
discriminatory slur, or match fixing. The sanction is a
suspension of between two and twelve matches, plus 6 demerit
points, and may lead to immediate termination of the player's
contract.

6. DEMERIT POINT THRESHOLDS

Demerit points remain on a player's record for 24 months. On
reaching 4 demerit points a player is suspended for one match.
On reaching 8 demerit points the suspension is two matches, and
on reaching 12 demerit points the suspension is four matches.

7. HEARING PROCESS

A charge is heard by the Match Referee within 24 hours of the
end of the match. The player may be accompanied by a
representative of the franchise.

8. APPEALS

A player may appeal a Level 3 or Level 4 sanction to an
independent Appeals Commissioner within 48 hours of the
decision. Level 1 and Level 2 sanctions may not be appealed.
