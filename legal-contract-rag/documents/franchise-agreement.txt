FRANCHISE PARTICIPATION AGREEMENT
For RAG Training Purposes Only

Agreement ID: FA-2025-003

1. APPOINTMENT

The Premier Cricket League grants Chennai Chargers Cricket Club
the right to operate a franchise team in the League for the
term set out in Section 2.

2. TERM

This agreement runs for ten seasons beginning with the 2026
season, renewable for a further five seasons on terms agreed by
both parties.

3. FRANCHISE FEE

The Franchise pays the League an annual franchise fee of
250,000,000 Indian rupees, due on 31 January each year.

4. SALARY CAP

The total of all player retainers and match fees paid by the
Franchise in a single season may not exceed 900,000,000 Indian
rupees. Amounts paid under a mid season replacement contract
count towards the cap.

5. CENTRAL REVENUE SHARE

The Franchise receives an equal share of central broadcast and
sponsorship revenue, distributed within 60 days of the end of
each season.

6. HOME TERRITORY

The Franchise holds exclusive commercial rights in the state of
Tamil Nadu and may not stage a home match outside its territory
without League consent.

7. SQUAD COMPOSITION

Each squad must contain at least 18 and no more than 25
registered players, of whom no more than 8 may be overseas
players, and no more than 4 overseas players may appear in the
playing eleven.

8. BREACH OF THE SALARY CAP

A franchise exceeding the salary cap is fined twice the excess
amount and may have competition points deducted.

9. TERMINATION

The League may terminate this agreement on 90 days notice if the
Franchise fails to pay the franchise fee or becomes insolvent.
