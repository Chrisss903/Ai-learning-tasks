AMENDMENT NUMBER ONE TO PLAYER CONTRACT
For RAG Training Purposes Only

Amendment ID: AMD-PC-011-01

1. PURPOSE

This Amendment Number One modifies the Player Registration and
Services Contract PC-2026-011.

2. AMENDED RETAINER FEE

Section 4 of PC-2026-011 is amended. The annual retainer of
9,000,000 Indian rupees is replaced with an annual retainer of
13,500,000 Indian rupees, reflecting the Player's appointment as
vice captain.

3. AMENDED TERMINATION NOTICE

Section 8 of PC-2026-011 is amended. The notice period of
60 days is replaced with a notice period of 90 days.

4. EFFECTIVE DATE

This amendment takes effect on 1 January 2026 and applies from
the 2026 season onwards.

5. NO OTHER CHANGES

All other provisions of PC-2026-011, including the Match Fee of
150,000 Indian rupees in Section 5, remain unchanged.

6. ORDER OF PRECEDENCE

If this amendment conflicts with PC-2026-011, this amendment
controls for the sections it amends.
