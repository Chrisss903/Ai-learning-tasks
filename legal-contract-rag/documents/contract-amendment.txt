SAMPLE CONTRACT AMENDMENT
For RAG Training Purposes Only

Amendment ID: AMD-2026-001

1. PURPOSE

This amendment modifies specific provisions of the
Service Agreement SA-2026-001.

2. TERMINATION NOTICE

Section 5 of the original Service Agreement is hereby
amended.

The original termination notice period of 30 days is
replaced with a termination notice period of 60 days.

The updated requirement is therefore:

Either party must provide 60 days written notice
before terminating the agreement.

3. EFFECTIVE DATE OF AMENDMENT

This amendment becomes effective on October 1, 2026.

4. OTHER TERMS

All other provisions of the original Service Agreement
remain unchanged and continue to remain in effect.

5. PRIORITY

If there is a conflict between this amendment and the
original Service Agreement regarding the termination
notice period, this amendment shall take precedence.
