MID SEASON PLAYER TRANSFER AGREEMENT
For RAG Training Purposes Only

Agreement ID: TRF-2026-008

1. PARTIES AND PLAYER

This agreement records the transfer of Arjun Kale, a wicket
keeper batter, from Deccan Falcons Cricket Club to Chennai
Chargers Cricket Club.

2. TRANSFER FEE

Chennai Chargers Cricket Club pays Deccan Falcons Cricket Club a
transfer fee of 35,000,000 Indian rupees within 21 days of the
League approving the transfer.

3. EFFECTIVE DATE

The transfer takes effect on 5 April 2026, after which the
Player is eligible to be selected by Chennai Chargers Cricket
Club.

4. OUTSTANDING PAYMENTS

Deccan Falcons Cricket Club remains liable for all retainer and
match fee payments accrued before the effective date.

5. MEDICAL CLEARANCE

The transfer is conditional on the Player passing a medical
examination arranged by Chennai Chargers Cricket Club. If the
Player fails the medical, this agreement lapses and no transfer
fee is payable.

6. SALARY CAP TREATMENT

The Player's remaining retainer counts towards the Chennai
Chargers salary cap under Section 4 of FA-2025-003 for the
portion of the season after the effective date.

7. SELL ON CLAUSE

If the Player is transferred again within 24 months, Deccan
Falcons Cricket Club receives 15 percent of any amount exceeding
35,000,000 Indian rupees.
