STADIUM HIRE AND MATCH DAY AGREEMENT
For RAG Training Purposes Only

Agreement ID: VEN-2026-005

1. PARTIES

This agreement is entered into between the Tamil Nadu Cricket
Stadium Trust (the "Venue") and Chennai Chargers Cricket Club
for the staging of home matches.

2. HIRE PERIOD

The Venue is made available for each home match from 48 hours
before the scheduled start until 12 hours after the close of
play.

3. HIRE CHARGES

Hire charges for each match are set out in Exhibit B. The
charges in Exhibit B are 4,000,000 Indian rupees for a league
match, 6,000,000 Indian rupees for a play-off match, and
9,000,000 Indian rupees for the final.

4. TICKETING REVENUE

Chennai Chargers Cricket Club retains 80 percent of net ticket
revenue for each home match and the Venue retains 20 percent.

5. CATERING AND CONCESSIONS

The Venue retains all catering and concession revenue and is
responsible for appointing vendors.

6. PITCH PREPARATION

The Venue prepares the pitch under the supervision of the League
curator. The Franchise may not direct pitch preparation.

7. SECURITY AND SPECTATOR SAFETY

The Venue provides stewarding and security to the standard
required by the League, and holds public liability insurance of
at least 500,000,000 Indian rupees.

8. CANCELLATION

If a match is abandoned without a ball being bowled, the hire
charge is reduced by 50 percent. If the match is abandoned after
play has started, the full hire charge is payable.
