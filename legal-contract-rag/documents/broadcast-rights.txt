MEDIA AND BROADCAST RIGHTS AGREEMENT
For RAG Training Purposes Only

Agreement ID: BRD-2026-002

1. GRANT OF RIGHTS

The Premier Cricket League grants Meridian Sports Network the
right to broadcast all League matches for the seasons covered by
this agreement.

2. TERRITORY

Television rights are granted for the Indian subcontinent.
Rights for all other territories are retained by the League and
are listed in Annex I.

3. DIGITAL AND STREAMING RIGHTS

Digital and mobile streaming rights are granted separately and
are described in Annex III, including the permitted number of
concurrent streams and highlight clip lengths.

4. RIGHTS FEE

Meridian Sports Network pays 4,500,000,000 Indian rupees per
season, payable in four equal quarterly instalments.

5. MINIMUM PRODUCTION STANDARDS

Every match must be covered by at least 24 cameras, including
ultra motion and stump cameras, and must be produced in high
definition with an ultra high definition feed available for the
final.

6. MINIMUM MATCHES

If fewer than 60 matches are staged in a season, the rights fee
is reduced proportionally for each match below that number.

7. ADVERTISING INVENTORY

Meridian Sports Network retains all in broadcast advertising
inventory except for 90 seconds per match reserved for League
promotional messages.

8. TERM

This agreement covers five seasons beginning with the 2026
season.
