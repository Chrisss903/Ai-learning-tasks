TITLE SPONSORSHIP AGREEMENT
For RAG Training Purposes Only

Agreement ID: SPN-2026-014

1. PARTIES

This sponsorship agreement is entered into between Chennai
Chargers Cricket Club and VoltEnergy Beverages Private Limited
(the "Sponsor").

2. SPONSORSHIP RIGHTS

The Sponsor receives front of shirt branding on all playing and
training kit, perimeter board branding at home matches, and
naming rights to the team's practice facility.

3. SPONSORSHIP FEE

The Sponsor pays 120,000,000 Indian rupees per season, payable
in two instalments, 60 percent before the first league match and
40 percent within 30 days of the final.

4. CATEGORY EXCLUSIVITY

The Sponsor holds exclusive rights in the energy drink and
sports hydration category. The Franchise may not sign another
sponsor in that category, and may not permit a player to appear
in a competing energy drink advertisement.

5. PLAYER APPEARANCES

The Sponsor is entitled to the player appearances and content
deliverables listed in Schedule B, which include 6 player
appearance days and 12 social media posts per season.

6. PERFORMANCE BONUS

If the Franchise wins the League final, the Sponsor pays an
additional 20,000,000 Indian rupees within 45 days of the final.

7. MORALITY CLAUSE

The Sponsor may suspend payment if a player brings the Sponsor
into disrepute through a Level 3 or Level 4 offence under the
Code of Conduct COC-2026.

8. TERM AND TERMINATION

This agreement covers the 2026 and 2027 seasons and may be
terminated by either party on 90 days written notice before the
start of a season.
