PLAYER REGISTRATION AND SERVICES CONTRACT
For RAG Training Purposes Only

Contract ID: PC-2026-027

1. PARTIES

This contract is entered into between Chennai Chargers Cricket
Club (the "Franchise") and Dinesh Mendis (the "Player"), an
overseas fast bowler registered with the Premier Cricket League.

2. DEFINITIONS

"Season" means the period from the first league match to the
final, including play-offs. "Match Fee" means the amount payable
for each match in which the Player is named in the playing
eleven. "Franchise Activity" means training, travel, media, and
sponsor appearances required by the Franchise.

3. CONTRACT TERM

This contract runs for one season only, covering the 2026
season, with an option to extend by mutual written agreement.

4. RETAINER FEE

The Franchise will pay the Player an annual retainer of
6,500,000 Indian rupees, payable in two equal instalments on
1 March and 1 June.

5. MATCH FEES

In addition to the retainer, the Player receives a Match Fee of
110,000 Indian rupees for each match in which he is named in the
playing eleven.

6. PLAYER AVAILABILITY

The Player must report to the team camp no later than 7 days
before the first league match of the Season, and must obtain a
no objection certificate from his home board before joining.

7. IMAGE RIGHTS

The Franchise may use the Player's name and likeness to promote
the Franchise and the League. The Player retains all existing
endorsement contracts declared before signing.

8. TERMINATION

Either party may terminate this contract by giving 30 days
written notice. The Franchise may also terminate if the Player
fails to obtain a no objection certificate from his home board.

9. INJURY AND MEDICAL

If the Player is injured during Franchise Activity, the
Franchise pays the retainer in full for the season. A
replacement player may be signed without further liability to
the Player.

10. CODE OF CONDUCT

The Player is bound by the Premier Cricket League Code of
Conduct COC-2026 and the Anti-Doping Policy ADP-2026.

11. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

This contract is governed by the laws of India, but any dispute
is referred to arbitration seated in Singapore under the rules
of the Singapore International Arbitration Centre.
