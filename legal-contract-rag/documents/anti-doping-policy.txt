ANTI-DOPING POLICY
For RAG Training Purposes Only

Document ID: ADP-2026

1. SCOPE

This policy applies to every player registered with the Premier
Cricket League and is based on the World Anti-Doping Code.

2. PROHIBITED SUBSTANCES

The list of prohibited substances and methods is set out in
Annex II, which is updated on 1 January each year.

3. REGISTERED TESTING POOL

Players named in the Registered Testing Pool must file
whereabouts information each quarter and keep it current. The
composition of the pool is published in Annex I.

4. WHEREABOUTS FAILURES

Three whereabouts failures within a 12 month period constitute
an anti-doping rule violation.

5. TESTING

Testing may be conducted in competition or out of competition,
with no advance notice. A player selected for testing must
report to the doping control station within 60 minutes.

6. PROVISIONAL SUSPENSION

Following an adverse analytical finding, the player is
provisionally suspended within 14 days pending the outcome of
results management.

7. THERAPEUTIC USE EXEMPTIONS

A player requiring a prohibited substance for a documented
medical condition must apply for a Therapeutic Use Exemption at
least 30 days before the substance is used.

8. SANCTIONS

A first violation involving a non specified substance carries a
period of ineligibility of four years, reduced to two years if
the player establishes the violation was not intentional.
