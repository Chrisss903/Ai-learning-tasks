PLAYER REGISTRATION AND SERVICES CONTRACT
For RAG Training Purposes Only

Contract ID: PC-2026-011

1. PARTIES

This contract is entered into between Chennai Chargers Cricket
Club (the "Franchise") and Rohan Sharma (the "Player"), a
domestic batting all-rounder registered with the Premier
Cricket League.

2. DEFINITIONS

"Season" means the period from the first league match to the
final, including play-offs. "Match Fee" means the amount payable
for each match in which the Player is named in the playing
eleven. "Franchise Activity" means training, travel, media, and
sponsor appearances required by the Franchise.

3. CONTRACT TERM

This contract runs for two seasons beginning with the 2026
season and ending after the 2027 final.

4. RETAINER FEE

The Franchise will pay the Player an annual retainer of
9,000,000 Indian rupees, payable in three equal instalments on
1 March, 1 May, and 1 July of each contract year.

5. MATCH FEES

In addition to the retainer, the Player receives a Match Fee of
150,000 Indian rupees for each match in which he is named in the
playing eleven. No Match Fee is payable for a match in which the
Player is named only as a reserve.

6. PLAYER AVAILABILITY

The Player must make himself available for all Franchise
Activity, and must report to the team camp no later than
14 days before the first league match of the Season.

7. IMAGE RIGHTS

The Franchise may use the Player's name and likeness to promote
the Franchise and the League. The Player keeps the right to
personal endorsements, provided they do not conflict with a
Franchise sponsor category under SPN-2026-014.

8. TERMINATION

Either party may terminate this contract by giving 60 days
written notice. The Franchise may terminate immediately for a
Level 4 offence under the Code of Conduct.

9. INJURY AND MEDICAL

If the Player is injured during Franchise Activity, the
Franchise pays the full retainer for that season and covers
medical treatment. An injury sustained outside Franchise
Activity reduces the retainer by 50 percent for the period the
Player is unavailable.

10. CODE OF CONDUCT

The Player is bound by the Premier Cricket League Code of
Conduct COC-2026 and the Anti-Doping Policy ADP-2026.

11. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

This contract is governed by the laws of India, and the courts
at Chennai have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute.
